## 3. Audit of verified evidence (precision)

The verifier proves a quote *exists* and *fits its stage*; it cannot prove the stage label is the best one or that the quote is about *this* project. So random verified evidence items were reviewed by hand: is the stage right, and is the quote about this project?

| | Round 1 (before fixes) | Round 2 (fresh sample, after fixes) |
|---|---|---|
| Fully correct | 25/40 (62.5%) | **31/40 (77.5%)** |
| Stage too low (conservative) | 7 | 4 |
| Stage too high (dangerous) | 2 | 2 |
| Not real evidence / wrong project | 6 | 3 |

Round 1 drove four rule fixes (network "interconnected" ≠ power; future tense never proves LIVE; upgrade an under-labelled event when its own quote states a higher stage; "TM Nxera" = Telekom Malaysia). Round 2 is a **fresh sample** that excluded every round-1 item, so its number is not inflated by tuning. Its findings ("seeks a loan", "will acquire land", "secured 264 acres", "solar deal with TNB") were then fixed and turned into regression tests. Most remaining errors are *conservative* — the system under-states a project's stage more often than it over-states it, the safe direction for a sales tool.

Labels are a first pass by an AI assistant (Claude) and should be confirmed by the team: `PHANTOM_AUDIT_SAMPLE_round1.csv`, `PHANTOM_AUDIT_SAMPLE_round2.csv`. The weakness still visible: a real quote about a *different* project in the same article (1–2 per 40).

## 6. Calibration backtest: does the score mean what it says?

Every project first seen by **2024-06-30** was re-scored using only the evidence published up to that date, then compared with what verified evidence shows since (regular hunt + Firecrawl outcome scout). *Progressed* = verified BUILDING or LIVE evidence; otherwise counted as not progressed.

**All 67 projects** — Brier score **0.097** (lower is better; always guessing the base rate of 33% would score 0.2205). 18 had news after the cutoff.

| Predicted score bucket | Projects | Mean predicted | Observed progressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-20% | 41 | 16% | 10% |
| 20-40% | 9 | 26% | 22% |
| 40-60% | 2 | 52% | 50% |
| 60-80% | 2 | 73% | 50% |
| 80-100% | 13 | 94% | 100% |

**The real test — 53 projects not yet under construction at the cutoff** — Brier 0.1199:

| Predicted score bucket | Projects | Mean predicted | Observed progressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-20% | 41 | 16% | 10% |
| 20-40% | 9 | 26% | 22% |
| 40-60% | 2 | 52% | 50% |
| 60-80% | 1 | 69% | 0% |

Limits: small sample; *not progressed* includes projects that may be progressing without coverage, so observed rates are a lower bound. With more history this table is how the layer priors would be re-fitted.

## 7. What is not measured yet (and how it would be)

- **A calibrated score at scale.** §6 is a first backtest on the projects old enough to judge; the layer priors are still stated assumptions until a few hundred outcomes exist to re-fit them.
- **Recall on small projects.** The reference list in §5 comes from published lists, which favour large operators; an analyst's full list for one region would measure the long tail.
